## 1. Strategy

ISO New Work Item Proposals are not submitted through contact forms. They advance through **Technical Advisory Group (TAG) membership**, **Working Group (WG) participation**, and **technical submissions** to national mirror committees. This document outlines the specific engagement path for each priority standards body.

---

## 2. Priority National Bodies

### 2.1 🇺🇸 ANSI — U.S. TAG to ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Organization** | American National Standards Institute (ANSI) |
| **TAG Administrator** | INCITS (InterNational Committee for Information Technology Standards) |
| **Mirror Committee** | INCITS/AI — Artificial Intelligence |
| **SC 42 Role** | P-member (Participating) |
| **Priority** | **Highest** — home country, most accessible entry point |

#### Engagement Path

1. **Join INCITS** — Apply for INCITS membership (organizational or individual)
   - Website: https://www.incits.org/participation/membership
   - Category: Technical Committee participant (TC fee applies)
   
2. **Join INCITS/AI** — Request assignment to the AI technical committee
   - This is the U.S. TAG to JTC 1/SC 42
   - Meetings are typically monthly (virtual + in-person at INCITS meetings)

3. **Submit NP through INCITS/AI** — Present DGI gap analysis to the TAG
   - Provide: DGI-TR-v1.0.md + DGI-Framework-GapAnalysis-v1.0.md
   - Request U.S. TAG support for NP ballot

4. **Attend SC 42 plenary** — As U.S. delegate (through INCITS/AI)
   - SC 42 plenaries are held ~2x/year (rotating locations)

#### Key Contacts to Identify

- INCITS/AI Chair and Vice-Chair
- U.S. Head of Delegation to SC 42
- NIST AI RMF team members who participate in SC 42 WGs

#### Timeline

| Action | Target Date |
|--------|:----------:|
| INCITS membership application | Q2 2026 |
| First INCITS/AI meeting attendance | Q2–Q3 2026 |
| DGI presentation to U.S. TAG | Q3 2026 |
| NP support request | Q4 2026 |

## 4. NP Submission Process

### 4.1 ISO/IEC JTC 1 NP Requirements

| Requirement | DGI Status |
|------------|:----------:|
| Project leader identified | ✅ Stuart Rainey |
| Scope statement defined | ✅ See DGI-TR-v1.0.md §1 |
| Non-duplication demonstrated | ✅ See NON-DUPLICATION-PROOF.md |
| International applicability | ✅ See GlobalApplicability-v1.0.md |
| ≥5 P-member national bodies willing to participate actively | 🔴 In progress |
| Project leader's national body support | 🟡 ANSI/INCITS — planned |
| Draft available (TR or WD) | ✅ DGI-TR-v1.0.md |

### 4.2 NP Ballot Process

1. Project leader's national body (ANSI/INCITS) submits NP to SC 42 Secretariat
2. NP distributed to all SC 42 P-members for 3-month ballot
3. P-members vote: approve, disapprove, or abstain
4. Approval requires:
   - ≥2/3 of P-member votes cast are affirmative
   - ≥5 P-members commit to active participation
5. If approved: Working Group established, WD development begins

### 4.3 Realistic Timeline

| Phase | Period | Milestone |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| **Preparation** | Q1–Q2 2026 | Finalize TR, gap analysis, contributor register |
| **U.S. TAG engagement** | Q2–Q3 2026 | Join INCITS/AI, present DGI |

| **NP submission** | Q1 2027 | Submit through ANSI/INCITS to SC 42 |

## 6. Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| NP rejected for insufficient support | Pre-engage ≥7 national bodies (buffer above minimum 5) |
| Scope overlap concern raised | Lead with NON-DUPLICATION-PROOF.md and SCOPE-BOUNDARIES.md |
| "Too product-specific" objection | DGI-TR-v1.0.md is completely product-neutral |
| Competing proposal from another body | Offer to merge/collaborate — DGI is implementation-neutral |
| Slow national body engagement | Start with ANSI (home body) + 2 most accessible international bodies |

---

## 7. Key Principle

> **Real influence happens through TAG membership, in-person WG meetings, and technical submissions — not contact forms.**

Every engagement should result in one of:
- A named expert willing to participate in the WG
- A national body willing to vote affirmatively on the NP ballot
- A technical review that strengthens the specification
