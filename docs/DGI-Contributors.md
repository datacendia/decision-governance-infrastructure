## 2. Expert Advisory — Seeking Contributors

> **Note to potential contributors:** DGI is seeking domain experts from at least 5 countries across academia, regulation, legal practice, and standards bodies to support ISO NP submission. If you are interested in contributing, please open a GitHub issue or contact contributors@datacendia.com.

### Desired Expert Profiles

ISO NP submissions are strengthened by endorsements from individuals with the following backgrounds:

| Role | Domain Expertise | Geographic Priority |
|------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **AI Governance Scholar** | AI ethics, algorithmic accountability, responsible AI | EU, Asia-Pacific |
| **Regulatory Expert** | EU AI Act, DORA, NIST AI RMF implementation | EU, US, UK |
| **Legal/Forensic Expert** | Digital evidence admissibility, e-discovery, litigation support | US, UK, Australia |
| **Cryptography Researcher** | Post-quantum cryptography, RFC 3161, blockchain timestamping | Any |
| **Standards Contributor** | ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 participation, ISO TC 307 (blockchain) | Any (P-member countries preferred) |
| **Financial Regulation** | Basel III/IV, DORA, BCBS 239, FATF compliance | EU, Singapore, Japan |
| **Healthcare Regulation** | FDA AI/ML, HIPAA, MDR, clinical decision support | US, EU |
| **Data Protection Authority** | GDPR enforcement, cross-border transfer mechanisms | EU, UK, Brazil, India |
| **Insurance/Actuarial** | AI risk underwriting, governance-based premium models | US, UK, Bermuda |
| **Auditor/Assessor** | ISO 42001 certification, SOC2 assessment, AI auditing | Any |

### Minimum Geographic Distribution for NP Submission

ISO requires support from multiple national bodies. Priority countries (P-members of JTC 1/SC 42):

| Region | Target Countries | SC 42 Status |
|--------|-----------------|:------------:|
| **Europe** | Germany (DIN), France (AFNOR), UK (BSI), Netherlands (NEN) | P-members |
| **North America** | United States (ANSI), Canada (SCC) | P-members |
| **Asia-Pacific** | Japan (JISC), South Korea (KATS), China (SAC), Singapore (ESG), Australia (SA) | P-members |
| **Other** | Brazil (ABNT), India (BIS), South Africa (SABS) | P-members |

**Minimum for NP approval:** Support from ≥5 P-member national bodies willing to participate actively.

## 4. Types of Contribution

| Contribution Level | Description | Time Commitment |
|-------------------|-------------|:--------------:|
| **Endorser** | Public statement of support for DGI standardization | Minimal |
| **Reviewer** | Review and comment on DGI specification drafts | 2–4 hours/quarter |
| **Working Group Member** | Active participation in ISO working group meetings | 4–8 hours/month |
| **Editor/Co-Author** | Contribute to specific sections of the specification | 8–16 hours/month |
| **National Body Delegate** | Represent a P-member national body in SC 42 | Per national body requirements |
